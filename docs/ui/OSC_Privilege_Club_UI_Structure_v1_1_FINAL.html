Rules currently in force
  • £1 = 1 point on all qualifying items — including sale, discounted and promotional

  • 100 points = £5 voucher value, rounded down
  • Redemption capped at £50 per order, in £5 increments

How to read this document. These are illustrative structure screens, produced to agree layout, navigation and field-level content before design begins. Visual styling, OSC brand treatment and final wording will be applied at the design stage. Every screen carries the proposal module number it delivers, so this pack can be reviewed alongside the Scope of Work.

What changed in v1.1, following Proposal v1.1 (14 Aug):
1. Email is now the primary customer identifier; postcode is a supporting field for lookup and duplicate validation. Search order, enrolment forms, duplicate checks and migration matching all reflect this.
2. Points are earned on all qualifying items, including sale, discounted and promotional lines. The previous full-price-only qualification logic has been removed throughout.
3. New: 30-day pending period. Earned points are held pending before becoming available or triggering any customer communication. Visible in admin balances, the ledger, the POS member view and the Klaviyo triggers.
4. Redemption is now value-based, not voucher-based at POS. Staff choose an amount in £5 increments, capped at £50 per order, above the minimum basket spend and below the basket total, with excluded products reducing the eligible portion.
5. Rules engine extended with minimum basket spend, per-order cap, increment, eligible/excluded items, and separate online and POS redemption switches.
6. Reports extended with channel, store, staff and gender filters, loyalty activity as a percentage of total sales by revenue and order count, and month-on-month / year-on-year comparison.
